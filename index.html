Conas í a léamhHow to read the map

Each dot is a Marine Institute phytoplankton monitoring station in a shellfish production area. Its colour shows the chance that Dinophysis, the dinoflagellate that is the main source of diarrhetic shellfish poisoning (DSP) toxins in Irish shellfish, reaches the 100 cells/L trigger level there in the next two weeks. A new outlook is issued every week. These are cell-count forecasts, not toxin results.

  • Lowunder 5%
  • Watch5–15%
  • Elevated15–30%
  • High30% or more
  • Paler, dashedno sample yet this week

An séasúrThe season at a glance

Every spoke is one week of the year. Its length shows how often a monitoring site saw Dinophysis reach 100 cells per litre within the following fortnight, across the 2016–2026 season replay on the map.

  • Peak season: early June to mid-July, when about one in five site-weeks had a bloom ahead.
  • First bloom of the season: usually mid to late June (median week 25; middle half weeks 22–28, across 661 area-seasons since 2003).
  • This week is marked on the dial.

An raibh a fhios agat?Did you know?

90% vs 2%A Monday sample is public in time for Sunday’s outlook 90% of the time; a Thursday sample just 2%. The day of sampling matters more than turnaround time. “+40%” → no effectThe popular “40% more blooms in heatwaves” disappears once you compare the same site in the same month. +82%Inside sheltered shellfish bays, temperature loggers record a median 82% more heatwave days than satellites see. 15.3% vs 3%When mussels are over the toxin limit, oysters are too 15.3% of the time in France, but only 3% in Ireland.

What we can say

  • The weekly outlook ranks the coming fortnight’s bloom weeks better than simply going by the usual pattern for each site and time of year.
  • The signal comes from recent plankton counts at and near each site.
  • Sea temperature, heatwaves, wind, chlorophyll, currents and rivers did not add to it.

Results in the wiki →

What we can’t say (yet)

  • It does not catch more shellfish closures than a simple trigger-level rule (≥100 cells/L at the site last week); its edge is far fewer false alarms.
  • So far it has been tested on past seasons. Live scoring starts 25 Oct 2026, with 2027 as the confirmatory season.

FúinnAbout

PA-Marine-Model is a research prototype by Garry Lohan and Felix Sproll. It is not an official product or official advice; shellfish safety is decided by official monitoring.

Data source: Marine Institute national phytoplankton and biotoxin monitoring programme, via MI ERDDAP (phytoplankton counts CC BY 4.0; biotoxin and status data free to use under the ERDDAP terms, not for legal use). Also E.U. Copernicus Marine Service information and other open data. Beyond the live map, this site shows national, regional and yearly aggregates only. Data sources & licences · Credits
